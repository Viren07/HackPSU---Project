# Real trial figures used for testing

These figures are reproduced from published clinical trials for non-commercial,
educational testing of PriorArt Lens (HackPSU Fall 2026). All rights remain with
the original publishers and authors.

| File | Trial | What the figure shows | Population | Reported HR (95% CI) | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `checkmate017.png` | CheckMate 017: nivolumab vs docetaxel | Overall survival (Figure 1) | Squamous NSCLC | 0.59 (0.44-0.79) | Brahmer J, et al. Nivolumab versus Docetaxel in Advanced Squamous-Cell Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer. *N Engl J Med* 2015;373:123-135. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1504627 |
| `OAK.png` | OAK: atezolizumab vs docetaxel | Overall survival, squamous subgroup (panel C) | Squamous NSCLC | 0.73 (0.54-0.98) | Rittmeyer A, et al. Atezolizumab versus docetaxel in patients with previously treated non-small-cell lung cancer (OAK). *Lancet* 2017;389:255-265. |
| `Orient-3.png` | ORIENT-3: sintilimab vs docetaxel | **Progression-free survival** (not overall survival) | Squamous NSCLC | PFS 0.52 (0.39-0.68); OS reported as 0.74 (0.56-0.96) | Shi Y, et al. Sintilimab versus docetaxel as second-line treatment in advanced or metastatic squamous non-small-cell lung cancer (ORIENT-3). *Cancer Communications* 2022;42(12):1314-1330. |
| `S-1.png` | East Asia S-1 Trial in Lung Cancer: S-1 vs docetaxel | Overall survival (panel A) | All NSCLC types (not squamous-only) | 0.945 (0.833-1.073) | Nokihara H, et al. Randomized controlled trial of S-1 versus docetaxel in patients with non-small-cell lung cancer previously treated with platinum-based chemotherapy (East Asia S-1 Trial in Lung Cancer). *Annals of Oncology*. |

## Notes
- The squamous comparison (nivolumab vs atezolizumab through docetaxel) uses `checkmate017.png` and `OAK.png`.
- `Orient-3.png` is a progression-free survival figure, so it cannot be combined with the overall survival figures. The ORIENT-3 overall survival figure is needed for that.
- `S-1.png` includes all NSCLC histologies, so it is used only to test the figure reader, not in the squamous comparison.
